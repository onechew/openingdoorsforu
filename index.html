FAQ

Answers for buyers, sellers, and renters across Etobicoke, Toronto, and the GTA.

Buying

Should I buy first or sell first?

The right strategy depends on your financial situation, market conditions, and comfort with risk. Some clients benefit from selling first for certainty, while others prefer buying first to secure their next home. I help create a plan based on your goals rather than using a one size fits all approach.

How much down payment do I need?

The required down payment depends on the purchase price of the property and current mortgage lending rules in Canada. I can connect you with trusted mortgage professionals who can explain your options and help you understand what fits your budget.

Do I need a mortgage pre approval before touring homes?

It is strongly recommended. A pre approval helps you understand your budget, strengthens your offer, and allows you to move quickly when the right property becomes available.

How long does closing take?

Most residential transactions in Ontario close within thirty to ninety days, although the timeline depends on the agreement between the buyer and seller. Every transaction is different, and I will help keep you informed throughout the process.

What is a status certificate?

A status certificate provides important information about a condominium corporation, including finances, fees, rules, and any legal matters. It helps buyers understand the condition of the condo corporation before completing the purchase.

How much are closing costs?

Closing costs vary depending on the property and buyer. They may include land transfer tax, legal fees, title insurance, adjustments, and other transaction related expenses. Buyers should budget beyond the purchase price.

What is land transfer tax?

Land transfer tax is paid when purchasing property in Ontario. Buyers in Toronto may also pay Toronto Municipal Land Transfer Tax in addition to the provincial tax. First time home buyers may qualify for rebates.

Should I buy a condo or a house?

It depends on your lifestyle, budget, maintenance preferences, and long term goals. Condos often require less maintenance, while freehold homes typically provide more space and flexibility.

What should I look for during a home showing?

Pay attention to the overall condition of the property, layout, natural light, storage, neighbourhood, future maintenance needs, and any signs of structural or moisture related issues. I also point out details buyers may overlook.

Can I buy and sell at the same time?

Yes. Many homeowners coordinate both transactions together. Timing, financing, and market conditions all play a role. I help create a strategy that minimizes stress and unnecessary risk.

Can foreign buyers purchase property in Canada?

Eligibility depends on current federal and provincial regulations. I can explain the current rules and connect you with legal professionals when additional advice is required.

What are condo maintenance fees?

Condo maintenance fees cover shared building expenses such as common area upkeep, amenities, insurance for the corporation, and reserve fund contributions. Fees vary by building and should be reviewed with the status certificate before you buy.

Is now a good time to buy a home in Toronto?

It depends on your personal timeline, financing, and the neighbourhood you are targeting. Toronto conditions shift by pocket and price point. I review current local inventory, recent sales, and your goals so you can decide with clarity rather than headlines.

Selling

What is the difference between list price and sale price?

The list price is the price chosen when a property is marketed. The final sale price is the amount agreed upon by the buyer and seller. Depending on market conditions, a home may sell above, below, or at the asking price.

Should sellers renovate before listing?

Not every renovation increases value. I help sellers identify improvements that are most likely to make a positive impact, while avoiding unnecessary expenses that may not provide a return.

How do I get a home valuation?

I provide complimentary home evaluations based on comparable sales, current market conditions, property features, and neighbourhood trends. This provides a more accurate estimate than relying solely on online value calculators.

How do I prepare my home for sale?

Preparing a home often includes decluttering, cleaning, staging, professional photography, pricing strategy, and marketing. The goal is to present the property in its best possible light to attract qualified buyers.

How much is my South Etobicoke home worth?

Value is driven by recent comparable sales, condition, renovations, and micro-location within South Etobicoke. I provide a complimentary evaluation based on those factors so you get a realistic range rather than a generic online estimate.

Market & Local

Is the GTA one housing market?

No. Every neighbourhood performs differently. Conditions in South Etobicoke may differ significantly from North York, Markham, or Vaughan. Local knowledge is important when making buying or selling decisions.

How competitive is the Toronto housing market?

Market conditions change throughout the year and vary by neighbourhood. Some communities experience multiple offers, while others provide more room for negotiation. I help clients understand current local conditions before making decisions.

What are the best neighbourhoods in South Etobicoke?

Popular communities include Mimico, Long Branch, Alderwood, New Toronto, Stonegate Queensway, and Humber Bay. Each offers a unique mix of housing styles, amenities, schools, and lifestyle.

Is South Etobicoke a good place to live?

South Etobicoke offers waterfront parks, established neighbourhoods, convenient transit, strong community amenities, and easy access to downtown Toronto. It is popular with families, professionals, and downsizers.

What are the best schools in South Etobicoke?

School options vary by catchment and grade level across Mimico, Long Branch, Alderwood, New Toronto, and nearby pockets. I help families understand the local school landscape and how it may affect neighbourhood choice, then connect you with current resources before you write an offer.

What makes South Etobicoke different from other Toronto neighbourhoods?

South Etobicoke combines waterfront living, established residential streets, and relatively quick access to downtown, with distinct communities like Mimico, Long Branch, and Alderwood. Housing mix, lifestyle, and pricing can feel very different from midtown or North York, which is why local guidance matters.
