About Grand Harbour

Developed by Rylar Developments in the early 1990s (with architecture often credited in the Beaux-Arts / postmodern vein associated with Matsui Baer Vanstone), Grand Harbour is a trio: two towers plus townhomes. The signature arch connecting the taller buildings is still one of the most recognizable silhouettes on this stretch of Lake Shore.

Architecture and setting

Unlike many later Humber Bay towers, Grand Harbour leans classic and community-scaled: courtyard and garden spaces, lakeside presence, and a design language that reads European rather than purely contemporary glass. Addresses span 2285 and 2287 Lake Shore for the towers and 2289 for the townhomes, confirm corporation and management contacts for the specific building you are buying.

Amenity depth

Residents typically look for indoor pool, hot tub and sauna, fitness, guest suites, 24/7 concierge and security, party/lounge space, courtyard gardens, visitor parking, and practical extras like an on-site car wash. The programming supports year-round living, not just a lobby checklist.

Why buyers still shortlist it

Value versus newer, smaller waterfront suites; larger layouts; and a Mimico lakeside lifestyle with trail and park access. It tends to appeal when someone wants character and space without giving up concierge-level convenience.

Suite features & layouts

Suite sizes historically span from compact plans through expansive multi-bedroom layouts (roughly mid-600s into the 3,000+ sqft range depending on tower and townhome). One-beds often live larger than many new builds; two-beds and den plans, three-beds, and townhomes give families and downsizers real options, always verify current measurements and fee inclusions.

About the neighbourhood

Grand Harbour at 2285 Lake Shore Blvd W sits in Mimico, one of South Etobicoke’s most watched waterfront condo pockets. Mimico blends an older lakeside village feel with loft and mid-rise living near Lake Shore Blvd W. Buyers get waterfront trail access, neighbourhood schools, and a slightly quieter pace than the densest Humber Bay towers, while still staying close to the Gardiner and downtown.

Nearby parks: Manchester Park, Grand Avenue Park and Platsis Parkette.

Waterfront and parks

Mimico Waterfront Park, Amos Waites Park, and the broader trail network give residents shoreline walks without needing a car. The lake edge and local greenspace are a big part of why families and downsizers shortlist this pocket.

Village amenities

Independent cafés, restaurants, and everyday services along Lake Shore and into Mimico Village support a neighbourhood lifestyle. Transit and highway access keep the rest of the city close when you need it.
