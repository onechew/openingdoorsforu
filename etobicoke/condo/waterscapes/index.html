About Waterscapes

Completed in the early 2010s era of Humber Bay intensification, Waterscapes pairs a distinctive rounded tower form with podium amenities and suite sizes that range from compact to expansive. It is a frequent comparison building for buyers touring Marine Parade.

Lifestyle amenities

Expect concierge, indoor pool, fitness and yoga space, theatre, lounge/party programming, guest suites, car wash, and outdoor BBQ/terrace living. The building is designed around daily use of shared spaces, not just a sales-centre amenity list.

Location edge

Marine Parade puts you in the walkable core of Humber Bay Shores, parks, trail, and neighbouring towers within a few minutes, with Lake Shore retail and transit nearby.

Suite features & layouts

Suite inventory spans tight junior layouts through large multi-bedroom residences. Orientation and floor matter enormously for lake vs city views; compare fees and parking carefully against neighbouring Marine Parade and Annie Craig towers.

About the neighbourhood

Waterscapes at 80 Marine Parade Dr sits in Humber Bay, one of South Etobicoke’s most watched waterfront condo pockets. Humber Bay Shores sits on Lake Ontario between the Humber River and Mimico Creek. It is a waterfront pocket with trail access, parkland, and a short drive downtown. Condos here trade on lake views, newer amenity programming, and day-to-day convenience along Lake Shore and Marine Parade.

Nearby parks: Humber Bay Shores Park, Palace Pier Park and Humber Bay Park East.

Waterfront living

Residents use the waterfront trail for walking, running, and cycling, with Humber Bay Park East/West, Palace Pier Park, and shoreline lookouts minutes from most towers. Marinas, green space, and open lake views are part of the everyday rhythm, not just weekend destinations.

Dining and daily life

Cafés, casual restaurants, and grocery options cluster along Lake Shore Blvd W and the Marine Parade strip, with more choice toward Mimico Village. Errands are doable on foot in many buildings, while the Gardiner and streetcar keep downtown and midtown reachable without living in the core.
